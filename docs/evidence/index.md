# Evidence

What other people's glTF assets actually contain, measured criterion by criterion rather than asserted.

This section is raw, and should be read that way. The assessment mixes measured results with ideas about what to do next, its corpus definition has moved twice, and some counts in the prose are stale against the current corpus. The profile's rules cite specific measurements from it; they do not rest on its narrative.

The measurement itself lives beside its inputs in [`assess/`](https://github.com/HonuRobotics/gltf-robotics/tree/main/assess), so that the generated report and the hand-edited criteria it answers stay together:

- `criteria.md` — what is assessed and why, criterion by criterion
- `examples.yaml` — the asset registry
- `findings.md` — what the corpus says, written by hand
- `ASSESSMENT.md` — generated by `gltf-assess`, never hand-edited

- [Community exemplars](community-exemplars.md) — who outside this project ships glTF to Gazebo and RViz, and what their files contain.
- [Parts library audit](audit-parts-library.md) and [BlueBoat chassis audit](audit-blueboat-chassis.md) — our own deliveries, measured against the same criteria.
- [Assessment brief](assessment-brief.md) — the question that started the corpus.
