Reference

Background the profile’s rules rest on, and the material a reader needs to argue with them.

  • Glossary — the vocabulary of the whole project, in one place.

  • Coordinate systems — what ISO 9787, the ROS REPs and glTF each say about frames, where they disagree, and which one a delivered model is expressed in. Claims are marked firm, practice, open or corrected.

  • Gazebo and RViz loader behavior — what Gazebo and RViz build from a .glb, read out of the gz-common and rviz_rendering sources. A snapshot from 2026-09-04, not re-verified since.

  • Blender glTF export options — all 110 properties of the export operator with their defaults, read out by introspection, and which ones the profile settles.

  • PBR and glTF — the metallic-roughness material model, and the difference between a property and a channel.

  • glTF background notes — reading notes and a reference collection, including the measured case for PNG over JPEG in the linear texture slots.
